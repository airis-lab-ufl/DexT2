DexT2 Synchronized Egocentric Vision, Pose & Bilateral Tactile Manipulation Dataset

28 manipulation tasks · Synchronized head & wrist RGB · 3D hand joints · High-density bilateral tactile sensing

4×7 Egocentric Task Wall (3s–8s active manipulation) · Scroll down ↓

DexT2 is an open egocentric visuo-tactile human manipulation dataset built to advance contact-rich Physical AI and dexterous robot learning. While vision-only first-person datasets struggle when fingers and tools occlude the point of contact, DexT2 captures fine-grained bimanual and tool-mediated manipulation—spanning precision tweezer handling, pipette and syringe operation, tube capping, zip-tie fastening, scissors cutting, deformable cloth folding, and container organization—paired with dense, hardware-synchronized contact feedback and 3D hand kinematics.

The curated release comprises 948 verified episodes across 29 manipulation tasks (4.57 hours / 274.4 minutes of active manipulation trajectories), exported in the standardized Open-X-Tactile / FTP-1 Zarr layout on the Hugging Face Hub. Every episode provides four synchronized sensing and annotation modalities: (1) Multi-view RGB video from an egocentric head camera and bilateral left/right wrist cameras, (2) 3D spatial & hand pose trajectories including 6-DoF head and wrist poses plus 21-joint 3D hand skeletons for both hands, (3) Bilateral high-density tactile sensing mapped across 15 anatomical hand regions per glove (distal/proximal phalanges and palm sub-areas), and (4) Human-verified subtask language instructions aligned at the segment level.
